## What this example shows

1. **One row per numbered book exercise, in book order.** The exercise number restarts at 1 in each new
   section (Grammar ex 1–6, then Vocabulary ex 1–2 on the same page). Page is the coursebook page.
2. **Kind = the book section the exercise sits in.** Every exercise in the Writing section is Writing,
   even when students are reading the model email. Grammar rows always fill `grammar` with the point
   being practised.
3. **The TB Warmer is folded into exercise 1** (in `note` and `self`). It is not a separate row.
4. **TB procedure becomes a short imperative `note`:** the one or two teacher moves that matter (lead-in
   question, concept check, "do 1st together", "model with example 0", "call out pairs to read answers",
   "ask Ss to predict"). Never the TB's full paragraph.
5. **`self` holds what he must remember in the moment:** answer keys (compressed, comma-separated),
   what students might not know, the TB's support tips, and, for a big final production task, the TB
   steps as a short checklist.
6. **Java's judgement beats the TB.** When the TB route is over-complicated he simplifies it and says so
   ("Teacher's Book complicates this. 1st let kids match…"). Prefer the simpler route for his groups.
7. **Who does it** follows the TB's main mode for the exercise. "Individually, then compare in pairs"
   counts as On their own. "As a class", "elicit" or "go through with the class" counts as All together,
   I lead.
8. **Minutes are small and realistic** (1–10), and the lesson fills about 80 minutes with the warm-up.
